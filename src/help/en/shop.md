# Shop

Spend the gold you win on ways to make the site your own. You get 100 for coming 1st in a game, 50 for 2nd and 20 for 3rd, and more for unlocking achievements.

Anything you own can be put on straight from here with **Use**. Animations are paused for people who have chosen to pause them in their profile, so those previews stand still.

## Names

Styles put a border and a background around your name, and animations move them, wherever other players see it.

## Backgrounds

Pictures go behind the site and the game tables. Animations play over them, and only you see those.

## Themes

Colours change every page on the site and fonts change the writing. Each is chosen separately and only changes how you see the site.

## Card backs

What the face-down cards look like to you in every card game.

## Avatar

Hair, clothes, hats and more for your avatar, shown in your details pop-up in games. Each part is previewed on you.

## Unlocks

Choose your own colour, gradient or picture, any marker colour for games, and set how strong and how fast your background animation is, on the Profile page.
