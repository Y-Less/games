## The idea

Dixnt is a card game for three or more friends. Every card is a picture, and every round starts with a word or phrase. You play the card from your hand that fits the word best, then everyone talks about the cards and works out who was bluffing.

## Starting a game

One player starts a new game and becomes the host. Everyone else joins with the three-word game code (such as `boss-aware-you`) or with the invite link. The host chooses the play style, the card categories, the hand size and the length of the game in the lobby, then starts the game once at least three players are there.

## How a round works

1. The **chooser** (the role passes round the table in the order players joined) types a word or phrase. The **Give me ideas** button suggests three.
2. Everybody else sees the word and plays one card from their hand that fits it. In the two imposter styles, one random player sees ??? instead of the word and has to bluff. You can swap your card until everyone has played.
3. All the cards and the real word are revealed. Explain why your card fits, and listen to everyone else.
4. The round is judged according to the play style (below).
5. Played cards are discarded, everyone draws back up to the hand size, and the next player becomes the chooser. When the draw pile runs out, the discard pile is shuffled and used again.

## Play styles

The host picks one of three styles in the lobby.

### Everyone votes

- Every player votes for whoever they think was in the dark. You cannot vote for yourself or for the chooser's card (the chooser plays none). You can change your vote until the last one is in.
- The group is right only if the imposter has strictly the most votes. A tie counts as wrong.
- If the group is right, each player who voted for the imposter scores 1 point.
- If the group is wrong, the imposter scores 1 point for every other player who did not vote for them.

### Only the chooser decides

- Only the chooser guesses who was in the dark, and that guess ends the round at once.
- If the chooser is right, they score 1 point.
- If the chooser is fooled, the imposter scores 5 points.

### Favourite card

- Nobody is kept in the dark: everyone sees the word.
- The chooser picks the card they like best, which ends the round.
- The player who played that card scores 2 points.

## Length of the game

The host decides how many times each player chooses the word (1 to 10, two by default). The game ends once everyone has had all their turns, and the player with the most points wins. If several players share the top score, they all win. A skipped round does not use up anyone's turn, and players who join late still get their turns, which makes the game longer.

## Card categories

The cards come in categories: Fantasy, Fairy tales, Modern world, Sci-fi, Steampunk and Victorian, Medieval, Games and Food. The host ticks which ones go into the deck. There must be enough cards for every player's hand.

## Good to know

- Players who join in the middle of a round are dealt in at the start of the next one.
- The host can remove players, skip a round (no points) or end the game, which returns everyone to the lobby with the scores reset. A game ended early does not count towards your statistics.
- If you lose your connection, just reopen the game: you carry on where you left off.
- A game closes when its last player leaves.
