# GTK

**G**etting **T**o **K**now, a game for 3 to 8 players.  Every round is about one of you.  They answer a couple of
questions about themselves honestly; everybody else makes up an answer to the very same questions.  Then the answers
go up together and you all try to spot the true one.

## Starting

- One player creates a game and shares the three-word code (or the invite link).  Everyone else joins with it.
- The host chooses how many rounds each player gets as the subject (1 to 3, normally 1), how many questions each round
  asks (1 to 4, normally 2), and how long everybody gets to answer and to pick (15 to 180 seconds, normally 60).
- Everybody is the subject the same number of times, in the order that people joined.  A game is over when the last
  round has been played.

## A round

1. One player is the subject.  Every question of the round is about them: they read "What is **your** favourite
   fruit?" and everybody else reads "What is **Ann's** favourite fruit?".
2. Everybody writes an answer to all of the round's questions at once, in secret.  The subject writes the truth;
   everybody else writes something they hope will pass for it.  Nobody sees anybody else's answers yet.
3. The answers to the **first** question go up together, in a shuffled order and with no names on them.  Everybody but
   the subject picks the one they believe is the subject's own.  You can never pick your own answer.
4. The true answer is revealed, with who wrote each of the others and who fell for them, and the points are given out.
5. The same happens for each of the round's other questions in turn.
6. Then the next player becomes the subject, with fresh questions.  No question is asked twice in a game.

## The clock

Writing your answers and picking one are both timed, and the countdown is in the Status box.  If the time runs out
before you have sent your answers, one is made up for you; if it runs out before you have picked, a pick is made for
you.  That way nobody who has wandered off can hold the table up.  The host sets how long it is.

## Scoring

- **Spotting the true answer:** 2 points.
- **Fooling somebody:** 1 point for every player who picks an answer you made up.  Three people taken in by one answer
  is 3 points.
- **Writing the truth by accident:** 1 point.  If what you made up comes to the same thing as the subject's own answer
  (ignoring capitals, punctuation and a plural "s"), the two are shown as one answer, you score for having guessed
  right, and you do not pick that question.
- The subject scores nothing in their own round, so everybody gets the same number of rounds off.  Getting away
  with it has its own reward: there is an achievement for a question of yours that nobody gets right.

The player with the most points at the end wins.  Tied leaders all win.

## Leaving and coming back

- If you lose your connection or close the page, just come back to the game's address.  You will find your game where
  you left it.
- Use **Leave game** to leave for good.  A computer player takes your place, keeps your score and answers for the
  round, and carries on; the host can also remove somebody who has gone away so that the game does not wait for them.
- If nobody is left, the game is thrown away and does not count towards statistics.
