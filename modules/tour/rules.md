# Tour

A tour is not a game.  It is an evening: you gather everybody once, and then the table votes on what to play, again
after every game, until you decide to stop.

## Starting

- One player makes a tour and shares the three-word code (or the invite link).  Everyone else joins with it, exactly as
  they would for any game.
- The host can add computer players, or fill the table up with one button, and starts the tour once at least two are
  there with at least one person among them.
- There is nothing to set.  What gets played is voted on, not chosen in the lobby.

## The vote

- Starting the tour opens the first vote, and every game that finishes opens another.
- A vote offers **three games at random**, out of every game on the site that the number of people here could actually
  sit down to — a table of nine is never offered a game for four.  When fewer than three suit, it offers all there are.
- The three are drawn afresh every time, and nothing is remembered: a game you played an hour ago can come round again,
  and the second vote is not the first vote's three.
- There is always a fourth choice: **End the tour**.
- Everybody votes, computer players included, and you can change your mind until the vote closes.  Computer players
  only ever vote for a game — when to stop is for the people at the table.  A vote somebody cast before they left
  stays cast, though: the computer player that takes their place keeps it, a vote to end included.
- The vote closes as soon as everybody has voted, or after a minute, whichever comes first.  The most votes wins and a
  tie is settled at random.  A vote nobody answers falls to one of the games, never to ending.

## Playing

- The winning game opens with everybody from the tour already at its table, and you are taken to it.
- Play it as you normally would: it is an ordinary game of that game, and it counts towards your statistics and
  achievements exactly as it would if you had started it yourself.
- When it ends, the tour opens the next vote and says so, wherever on the site you are.
- A tour keeps no score of its own.  Each game keeps its own, as it always has.

## Ending

- The tour ends when **End the tour** wins a vote.  The host can then start another with the same people.
- If a game somehow cannot be opened, the tour says so and votes again rather than ending your evening.

## Leaving and coming back

- The tour is a room like any other: come back to its address and you will find it where you left it.
- Leaving the tour does not take you out of a game it sent you to, and leaving that game does not take you out of the
  tour.  They are separate rooms, and you can be in both.
