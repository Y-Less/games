# Just One

A game for 3 to 7 players. One player, the **guesser**, tries to find a secret word from clues that everybody else writes. The clues that are too alike are thrown out, so the best clue is one that nobody else thinks of.

## Starting

- One player creates a game and shares the three-word code (or the invite link). Everyone else joins with it.
- The host chooses how many turns each player gets as the guesser (1 to 5, normally 2) and starts the game.
- A game is over when everyone has been the guesser that many times. Turns go round in the order that people joined.

## A turn

1. The guesser does not see the secret word. Everyone else does.
2. Every other player secretly writes **one word** as a clue, and sends it. A clue must be a single word, and not the secret word or part of it. Nobody sees anybody else's clue yet.
3. When all the clues are in, any that match are **cancelled**, every one of them: "Apple" and "apples" are the same clue, and so are two people who both write "red". The other players can see which were cancelled.
4. The guesser sees the clues that are left, in a shuffled order and without their writers, and makes **one guess**. They may pass instead.
5. If the guess is right and some clues were left, the guesser picks the **best hint** from them.
6. The word is revealed with every clue and who wrote it. Everyone closes the scores pop-up to say they are ready; when all of them have, the next turn starts (or the game ends after the last turn).

## Scoring

- **Right guess:** the guesser scores **2 points**, and the writer of the best hint scores **1 point**. Nobody else scores.
- **Wrong guess, or a pass:** nobody scores.
- If every clue was cancelled and the guesser still gets the word, they score their 2 points and there is no best hint.

The player with the most points at the end wins. Tied leaders all win.

## Leaving and coming back

- If you lose your connection or close the page, just come back to the game's address. You will find your game where you left it.
- Use **Leave game** to leave for good. If the guesser leaves, that turn is skipped. The host can remove a player who has gone away so that the game does not wait for them.
- If fewer than two players are left, the game is abandoned and does not count towards statistics.
