# Hearts

A game for three to seven players (four is the usual number). Nobody wants points: the lowest score wins.

## Starting

- One player creates a game and shares the three-word code (or the invite link). The host fills the other seats with friends or **computer players**, chooses the settings and starts the game once every seat is taken. The host chooses the **number of players** (3 to 7, normally 4) in the lobby, before the game starts.
- To deal evenly, a few low cards are taken out of the pack first, and then everybody is dealt the same number of cards. The Queen of Spades and all the hearts are always kept. The game is played over several hands until somebody reaches the target score.

| Players | Cards removed | Cards each (and tricks) | Cards passed | Passing, hand by hand |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 3 | 2 of clubs | 17 | 3 | left, right, none |
| 4 | none | 13 | 3 | left, right, across, none |
| 5 | 2 of clubs, 2 of diamonds | 10 | 3 | 1 to the left, 2 to the left, 2 to the right, 1 to the right, none |
| 6 | 2 and 3 of clubs, 2 and 3 of diamonds | 8 | 2 | 1, 2, 3 (across), 4, 5 places to the left, none |
| 7 | 2 of clubs, 2 and 3 of diamonds | 7 | 2 | 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 places to the left, none |

"1 to the left" is the next player in turn; "2 to the right" is the same as "3 to the left" with five players, and so on.

## Passing

- Before each hand, everyone chooses cards and passes them (see the table: three cards, or two with six or seven players). With four players that is to the **left** on the first hand, to the **right** on the second, **across** on the third, and not at all on the fourth. With other numbers of players the cards travel further each hand, as the table shows, and the last hand of each round is always played with no passing. Then the pattern repeats. The table shows who you pass to and who you receive from.
- You choose your three cards without seeing what you will receive.

## Playing a hand

- The player holding the **lowest club left in the pack** (the 2 of clubs, or the 3 of clubs when the 2 has been taken out, and so on) leads it to start the first trick.
- You must **follow suit** if you can. If you cannot, you may play any card.
- The highest card of the suit led wins the trick, and its winner leads the next one.
- You cannot lead a heart until hearts have been **broken** (a heart or the Queen of Spades has been played), unless you hold nothing but hearts.
- On the first trick you cannot play a heart or the Queen of Spades, unless every card you hold is one of those.

## Scoring

- Every **heart** you take in tricks is **1 point**. The **Queen of Spades** is **13 points**. There are always 26 points in a hand, whatever the number of players.
- **Jack of Diamonds:** unless the host turns it off, the game makes the **Jack of Diamonds** worth **-5** to whoever takes it. This is on by default; the host can turn it off.
- **Shooting the moon:** a player who takes **all 13 hearts and the Queen of Spades** shoots the moon. The host chooses what happens: by default **every other player scores 26 points** and the shooter scores nothing, or the shooter scores **-26** instead and everyone else scores nothing.
- When the Jack of Diamonds rule is on, shooting the moon still only needs the hearts and the Queen of Spades; the Jack does not have to be taken. The Jack **still counts** on a moon hand: whoever took it scores **-5** on top of the moon scores. For example, another player who took the Jack scores 21 instead of 26, and a shooter who also took it scores -5 (or -31 in the -26 mode).

## Winning

- The game ends at the end of the first hand in which somebody has reached the **target score**: 50, 100 or 150 points (the host chooses, normally 100).
- The player with the **lowest** score wins. If the lowest score is tied, everyone on it wins.

## Leaving and coming back

- If you lose your connection or close the page, return to the game's address and you will find your game where you left it.
- If you leave a game that has started, a computer player takes over your seat and the game carries on for the others. You cannot return to it, and it does not count towards your statistics. If nobody is left, the game is dropped.
