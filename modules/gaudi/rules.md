# Gaudi

Build a stained-glass window from drafted dice. Two to five players, ten rounds, and the highest score wins.

## Starting

- One player creates a game and shares the three-word code (or the invite link). The host chooses **how many seats** there are (2 to 5), fills them with friends or **computer players**, and starts the game once every seat is taken.
- The host can switch on **Bad private scoring** in the lobby (see Private objectives). It is off by default, and everyone can see which rule applies.
- Everyone is privately dealt **four windows** (two double-sided cards). Choose one, all at the same time. Nobody sees the others' choices until everyone has chosen.
- Your window's **difficulty** (3 to 6) is how many **favour tokens** you receive.

## The dice

- The bag holds exactly the dice the game will use: ten rounds of (2 x players + 1) dice, shared evenly between the five colours. That is 10 dice of each colour for two players, 14 for three, 18 for four and 22 for five.
- Each round the first player rolls (2 x players + 1) dice from the bag. They form the **pool**. Every die shows a colour (and a shape, to help with colour-blindness) and a shade from 1 to 6.

## A round

- Each player takes **two turns** in snake order: clockwise from the round's first player, then back again. With three players that is A B C C B A.
- On your turn you may, in either order and each at most once, **place one die** from the pool and **use one tool card**. You can also do neither. Press **End turn** when you are finished. The turn never ends by itself: until you press End turn you may change your mind with **Undo turn**, which takes the whole turn back to how it began (your window, the pool, your favour tokens, any tool you used) so that you can choose differently. Undo never reaches back past End turn..
- After the last turn, the leftover dice go onto the **round track**, and the first-player marker moves one seat clockwise.

## Placing dice

- Your first die goes on an **edge or corner** space. Every later die must **touch** a die already in your window, sideways or diagonally.
- A coloured space only accepts that colour. A numbered space only accepts that shade. Blank spaces accept anything.
- No die may be **directly beside** (not diagonally) a die of the **same colour** or the **same shade**.

## Tool cards

- Three of the twelve tools are in play. Using a tool costs **1 favour token** the first time it is used by anyone, and **2 tokens** every time after that. The tokens stay on the card.
- Each tool is used in a single step, with everything it needs chosen first. If anything about it is not allowed, nothing happens and you keep your tokens.
- **Swift Hand** (on your first turn of a round only): when you end that turn you immediately take a second full turn, before anyone else moves: choose another die, place it and, if you wish, use another tool (paying favour tokens as usual). Your normal second turn of the round is then skipped, so you take two turns in a row.

## Objectives

- **Public objectives:** three are in play, and everyone scores them from their own window.
- **Private objective:** each player is secretly given one colour. By default you score the **shades of all the dice of that colour** in your window added together. If the host chose **Bad private scoring**, each of those dice is instead worth a flat **3 points**, whatever it shows.

## Scoring

- Your score is your **public objectives**, plus your **private objective**, plus **1 point per favour token** you still have, minus **1 point per empty space**.
- While the game runs, the scoreboard shows **provisional scores** (no private objective). Your own secret objective is shown only to you, and everyone's is revealed at the end.
- After round 10 the highest score wins. Ties are broken by the higher private objective score, then more favour tokens left, then the player who comes **last** in the final round's turn order. If that still does not settle it, the win is shared.
