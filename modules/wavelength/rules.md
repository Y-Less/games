# Wavelength

A game for 2 to 8 players. Somewhere between two opposites, such as **Cold** and **Hot**, there is a hidden spot on a dial. One player, the **psychic**, knows where it is and gives a clue. Everyone else tries to turn the dial to the same spot.

## Starting

- One player creates a game and shares the three-word code (or the invite link). Everyone else joins with it.
- Each player can pick a marker colour. The host chooses how many turns each player gets as the psychic (1 to 5, normally 2) and starts the game.
- A game is over when everyone has been the psychic that many times. Turns go round in the order that people joined.

## A turn

1. Everyone sees the two ends of the spectrum. Only the psychic sees where the hidden spot is.
2. The psychic types a clue: a word or a short phrase that points to that spot, such as "a hot bath" for a spot a little to the right of the middle between Cold and Hot. The clue should not use numbers or the words on the dial.
3. Everyone else turns the dial to where they think the spot is and locks in the guess. Guesses stay hidden until everyone has guessed.
4. The spot is revealed with every guess on the dial, and the points are handed out.
5. Everyone closes the scores pop-up to say they are ready; when all of them have, the next turn starts (or the game ends after the last turn).

## Scoring

The hidden spot is a narrow target made of three bands. A guess scores for the band it lands in:

- **4 points** for the middle of the target (a bullseye).
- **3 points** for the next band out on either side.
- **2 points** for the outer band.
- **0 points** for anything further away.

The psychic scores the average of the guessers' points, rounded to the nearest whole number. A good clue helps everybody.

The player with the most points at the end wins. Tied leaders all win.

## Leaving and coming back

- If you lose your connection or close the page, just come back to the game's address. You will find your game where you left it.
- Use **Leave game** to leave for good. If the psychic leaves, that turn is skipped. The host can remove a player who has gone away so that the game does not wait for them.
- If fewer than two players are left, the game is abandoned and does not count towards statistics.
