# Tints and Hints

A game for 2 to 8 players. One player secretly sees a colour on a grid and describes it. Everybody else tries to find that square.

## The grid

The grid has 48 columns (A to Z, then AA to AV, which run round the colour wheel) and 20 rows (1 to 20, from muted and dark, through vivid, to pale). A square is a column and a row, such as **M9** or **AC14**.

## Starting

- One player creates a game and shares the three-word code (or the invite link). Everyone else joins with it.
- Each player can pick a marker colour. The host chooses how many turns each player gets as the hinter (1 to 5, normally 2) and starts the game.
- A game is over when everyone has been the hinter that many times. Turns go round in the order that people joined.

## A turn

1. The **hinter** sees the secret square and gives a first hint, out loud or in the hint box. Hints should describe the colour, not name the square.
2. Everyone else picks a square and locks it in. You only get one guess a round. Guesses stay hidden from the other guessers until the round is closed.
3. When everyone has guessed, the hinter presses **Continue**. The first guesses are now visible to all, and the hinter gives a second hint to help.
4. Everyone guesses again. This time you can see where people guessed the first time.
5. The hinter presses **Continue** again. The secret square is revealed and the points are handed out.
6. The hinter (or the host) presses **Next turn**.

## Scoring

A guess is **close** if it is within one square of the target in any direction, diagonals included. The colours do not wrap round from AV to A.

- First round: **5 points** for the exact square, **2 points** for a close one.
- Second round: **4 points** for the exact square, **1 point** for a close one.
- The hinter scores **3 points for every player who found the exact square** (in either round, each player counts once).
- A player who guesses exactly right in both rounds scores for both.

The player with the most points at the end wins. Tied leaders all win.

## Leaving and coming back

- If you lose your connection or close the page, just come back to the game's address. You will find your game where you left it.
- Use **Leave game** to leave for good. If the hinter leaves, that turn is skipped. The host can remove a player who has gone away so that the game does not wait for them.
- If fewer than two players are left, the game is abandoned and does not count towards statistics.
