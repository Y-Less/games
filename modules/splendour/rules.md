# Splendour

An engine-building game for two to four players. Gems buy cards, cards give permanent discounts, and the discounts buy better cards. The first to reach the target prestige, once the round is finished, wins.

## Starting

- One player creates a game and shares the three-word code (or the invite link). The host chooses how many seats there are (2 to 4), fills them with friends or **computer players**, picks the target prestige (10, 15 or 20, usually 15) and starts the game once every seat is taken.
- There are five gem colours (Diamond, Sapphire, Emerald, Ruby, Onyx) and **Gold**, which is wild.
- Each colour has 4 tokens with two players, 5 with three and 7 with four. There are always 5 Gold.
- Four development cards of each of the three levels lie face up, with a face-down pile for each level. There is one more noble than there are players.

## Your turn

Do exactly one of these.

- **Take three gems** of three different colours. If fewer than three colours are left you may take fewer.
- **Take two gems** of the same colour, but only if at least four of that colour are in the supply.
- **Reserve a card.** Take any face-up card, or the top card of a pile without looking at it, and keep it in your hand. You also take one Gold if any is left. You may hold at most 3 reserved cards, and you can still reserve when there is no Gold left. Other players see how many cards you have reserved, but not the ones you took from a pile.
- **Buy a card.** Buy a face-up card, or one of your reserved cards, by paying its cost. Paid tokens go back to the supply and the gap in the table is filled from that pile.

## Bonuses and Gold

- Every card you own gives a permanent **discount of 1** in its colour on everything you buy later.
- Gold can pay for any one gem. You may choose to spend Gold even if you could pay with gems.
- If there is nothing at all you can do, you pass.

## Tokens

- You may hold at most **10 tokens** (Gold included) at the end of your turn. If you have more, you must return tokens of your choice straight away.

## Nobles

- At the end of your turn, if your cards give you enough bonuses for a noble, that noble visits you and is worth 3 prestige. Nobles are never bought or reserved.
- If several nobles qualify you choose one. Only one noble can visit you each turn.

## Winning

- Prestige comes from cards and nobles. When a player has reached the target at the end of their turn, the round is finished so that everybody has had the same number of turns.
- The player with the most prestige wins. A tie is broken by fewer development cards bought, and if that is also level the win is shared.
- A game that has dragged on for 100 rounds, or in which nobody can do anything any more, ends and is scored in the same way.

## Scores

- The bar at the top always shows everyone's prestige. After every round a pop-up lists each round so far, with the prestige each player gained in it.
