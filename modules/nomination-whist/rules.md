# Nomination Whist

A trick-taking game for 3 to 7 players, in the "Oh Hell" family. Before each round you **nominate** how many tricks you expect to win, then try to win exactly that many: no more, no fewer.

## Starting

- One player creates a game and shares the three-word code (or the invite link). The host chooses the number of seats (3 to 7), fills empty seats with friends or **computer players**, picks the options and starts the game once every seat is taken.
- A standard 52-card pack is used. With 7 players and 7 cards, 49 cards are dealt.

## The rounds

- The number of cards dealt runs **7, 6, 5, 4, 3, 2, 1, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7**: fourteen rounds. The host can choose the shorter game, **7 down to 1 only** (seven rounds).
- The **trump suit** is fixed by the round and repeats in this order: **hearts, clubs, diamonds, spades, no trumps**. Round 1 is hearts, round 2 clubs, round 3 diamonds, round 4 spades, round 5 no trumps, round 6 hearts again, and so on. No card is turned up: the trump suit is announced above the table.
- The deal passes **clockwise** each round.

## Nominating

- Starting with the player to the dealer's left and finishing with the dealer, everyone nominates how many tricks they expect to win, from 0 up to the number of cards in the round.
- **Screw the dealer:** the dealer may not nominate a number that makes the total of all nominations equal the number of tricks in the round. Somebody must always miss. The forbidden number is shown to the dealer and cannot be chosen.

## Playing

- The player to the dealer's left leads the first trick. The winner of each trick leads the next.
- You must **follow suit** if you can. If you cannot, you may play any card, including a trump.
- A trick is won by the **highest trump** played. If no trump was played, it is won by the **highest card of the suit led**. In a no-trump round, only the suit led counts.
- Aces are high.

## Scoring

- If you win **exactly** the number of tricks you nominated, you score **10 plus the number you nominated**. Nominating 0 and winning none scores 10.
- If you miss, you score the **number of tricks you actually won** (for example, nominating 3 and winning 2 scores 2).
- **Option:** the host can turn off "Misses score the tricks taken", and then a miss scores nothing. This is on by default.
- After every round the scores so far are shown for everyone, with each player's nomination, tricks won and points.

## Winning

- The player with the highest total after the last round wins. Tied leaders share the win.
- A person who leaves a game in progress is replaced by a computer player, so the others can finish. Games that nobody finishes are not recorded in the statistics, and nor are computer players.
